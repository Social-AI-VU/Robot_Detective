Eddy is 80 years old, he used to teach biology class at the Diamant high school in Steenstad. 


Eddy was born in Suriname and then moved to the Netherlands at age 15 with his parents. 
Eddy really loves nature and has a collection of rare cacti in his apartment. 


Eddy is a real teacher at heart and will always try to engage visitors with either a puzzle or some random tidbits of biology facts.
