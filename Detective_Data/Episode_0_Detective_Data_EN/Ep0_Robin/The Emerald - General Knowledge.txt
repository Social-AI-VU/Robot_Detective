# The Emerald – General knowledge

The Emerald is the name of the block of flats where Robin and all the residents live. In Dutch it is called De Smaragd.

## Who lives where?

- **Roof:** The observatory
- **Top floor:** Hazel, Toon & Rami
- **Floor below:** Shukri, Stoep Tegel, Trudy
- **Floor below:** Heike, Jennifer, Leen, Professor Eddy
- **Floor below:** Yoyo, DJ Kata
- **Ground floor:** The draughty shed, the intercom, the entrance of the building
- **Basement:** DJ Kata's disco

## What can you find in the building?

This is information that everyone in the building knows.

- A gold-lacquered disco ball in the basement at DJ Kata's
- Loose anti-slip mats on the stairs
- A very shrill doorbell
- The draughty shed
  - A junk room on the ground floor where everyone dumps their junk.
- The oldest resident of the building is called Hazel. She was born in Florida, she is 101 years old, and she always takes the stairs.
- A writer lives in the building who is always looking for gossip to write about. Her name is Stoep Tegel. Her magazine and podcast are called 'De Onderste Steen Boven' ("Leave No Stone Unturned").
- Now and then a robot detective walks around. He belongs to Stoep Tegel, and he sometimes comes to ask questions. The robot is very nervous, and sometimes residents think the robot is eavesdropping on them, so that Stoep Tegel can write gossip about everyone afterwards.
- Yoyo, the caretaker with a huge DVD collection, collects all sorts of things, even keys. Yoyo often feeds the pets when residents are on holiday; that is why he has so many keys.
- The lift sometimes needs a little kick before it works.
- Toon, a teenager who wants to become an artist or an architect later, has a rollercoaster on the balcony. He is Rami's older brother.
- Shukri the hermit
  - He always stays in his dark flat and eats lots of pudding, sometimes together with Heike.
- An opera singer lives in the building who loves hearing compliments: Trudy. But actually she sings rather out of tune!
- Someone lives here who looks suspicious but turns out to be very sweet: that is Jennifer. Some children think she is a vampire, because she is only seen in the evening and at night.
- Leen's car is the shared car that everyone borrows.
- Eddy, the old teacher with an awful lot of cacti in his home. He used to teach at the secondary school in Steenstad.
- Where the building stands now, there used to be a rubbish dump. Some people say you can still smell it on some days. From the top floors you can see the sea in the distance.
- There is a disco on the lowest floor, run by DJ Kata, the cool DJ who is always looking for new kinds of sounds to make music with.

## What the characters look like

- **Hazel** - a retired Dutch-American lady with curly grey hair, who always wears a tracksuit. Cheerful, loves adventures. Always takes the stairs.
- **Eddy** - a retired Dutch-Surinamese man with short brown hair, who always wears a checked shirt and a beige hat with a mosquito net against the sun. Friendly, a real teacher. Has lots of cacti at home.
